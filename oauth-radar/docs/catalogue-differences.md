# The scaffold against the owner's catalogue

**Date:** 2026-11-19

The [brief](../BRIEF.md) and decisions OAR-001 to OAR-007 were written on 2026-11-15 without a design from the owner (ruling R10 asked for one). The design now exists: the owner's portfolio catalogue entry for Shadow AI Radar, written 2026-11-08. It isn't in the repo. Where the two disagree, **the catalogue wins**, because no OAR decision has been accepted yet: all seven are still "Proposed".

This page lists every place they differ, and what milestone 1 does about each. Milestone 1 here is the owner's list of 2026-11-19 (collector, shared dataset grants, scope explainer, risk rubric, dry-run policy engine, Slack nudge drafts, weekly report, precision and recall), not the scaffold's milestone 1 ([numbering](#milestone-numbering)). Nothing below changes an OAR decision. Each one stays Proposed until the owner rules (R20), and a change to one is a new record that supersedes it.

## The differences

| # | Item | Scaffold says | Catalogue says | What milestone 1 does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where the design comes from | No catalogue exists; the brief is built from the lab brief and decisions only ([brief](../BRIEF.md), question 1) | The catalogue is the design (answers R10) | Follows the catalogue wherever the two differ, and lists each difference here |
| 2 | **The risk score** | [OAR-002](adr/002-risk-model-rules-first.md): additive points. The precision and recall report shows how OAR-002 does on the planted grants, so the owner can compare |

| 4 | Vendor maturity | Not a signal. Only "publisher verified or not" (+2) | A factor in the risk score; the enricher knows the vendor | Held in the tuning world's app catalogue and shown in the report; not scored, since OAR-002 doesn't have it |
| 5 | AI-app and "not reviewed" signals | +1 each in OAR-002 | Not in the rubric | Kept, as OAR-002 states |

| 7 | Who writes the explanation | No AI in milestones 0 to 3; an optional AI explainer in milestone 4 ([OAR-007](adr/007-rules-decide-ai-explainer-later.md)) | "AI may write the explanation, never the score" | No AI and no model call. | 8 | **Revoking** | [OAR-004](adr/004-read-only-never-revoke.md): read-only, never revoke; a finding can only ask for review; revoking would need a superseding ADR with a plan, a dry run, a blast-radius limit and an allowlist | The policy engine removes dormant restricted-scope grants after a **7-day notice**, and routes everything else to a human | A **dry-run** policy engine: a notice draft first, then, once seven days have passed on a simulated clock, a revoke **plan** (a reviewed file). | 9 | Emergency removal | None. | 11 | Blast radius | A precondition for any future revoking (OAR-004) | Principle 4: blast-radius limits stop mass changes | One plan may touch at most a fixed number of grants. A plan over the limit is refused whole, and its grants go to a person |
| 12 | Plan before apply, idempotent | Same input, same findings (OAR-002) | Principles 2 and 3: every change is a reviewed diff, dry-run by default, idempotent by design | Same inputs and the same simulated date give byte-identical plans, notices and drafts (tests) |

| 19 | **Metrics** | Recall on planted high-risk grants, target 100%; precision reported (the lab's target is ≥ 0.90); per-type numbers; 0 revokes; 0 API and AI calls | Planted risky grants found: 6 of 6.
